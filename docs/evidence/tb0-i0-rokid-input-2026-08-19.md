## Evidence provenance labels

Every input claim MUST carry exactly one provenance label:

- `PHYSICAL` — generated by a genuine Rokid hardware interaction while the current `dsh-glasses` APK and synchronized low-level/framework tracers were armed.
- `SYNTHETIC_ADB` — generated through Android `adb shell input` or another framework-level injection.
- `SYNTHETIC_SENDEVENT` — generated by writing Linux input events through a debug-only injection path. This proves reducer/wiring behavior, not physical hardware behavior.
- `PRIOR_REFERENCE` — evidence from official Rokid documentation or Poker-Dealer records on the same firmware/device family. It may inform hypotheses but cannot qualify this APK.

Only `PHYSICAL` evidence can close a physical-control row.

## Acceptance rules

A physical binding may be frozen only when a genuine interaction proves:

- complete BEGIN/UPDATE-or-HOLD/END-or-CANCEL lifecycle;
- stable raw event identity and Android mapping;
- timing sufficient to distinguish short, double, and long behavior where required;
- focus/background cancellation behavior;
- whether a conflicting Rokid-native operation also fires;
- reproducibility across at least three bounded trials without unexplained divergence.

Synthetic replay is accepted only for validating tracer/reducer plumbing. It must never be presented as hardware qualification.
